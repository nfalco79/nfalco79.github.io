# Recruiting Refugees

If you've faced the Fury Lord every day, on Tuesday you'll have at least 15.5k tickets (depending on your damage ranking, you may have more).
Each round, there are three refugees of various colors; every 10th recruits (not rounds), the system guaranteed one orange.
Scenarios:
- 3 greens, skip to the next round by paying 250 diamonds
- 2 greens, skip to the next round by paying 250 diamonds
- 1 green and no orange, spend 500 tickets and go to the next round for free
- 1 orange, if you already have the refugee (see the stars on their photo), you will only get orange refugee fragments, spend 500 tickets and go to the next round for free
  <image link>
- 1 orange, if the refugee is NOT an army trainer, butler, diplomat, scientist, or nurse, spend 500 tickets and go to the next round for free
- 1 orange, if the refugee is an instructor, housekeeper, diplomat, scientist, or infirmary, spend tickets until you get it and go to the next round for free
- 2 or 3 oranges in the same round, spend tickets until you have all orange refugees and go to the next round for free
