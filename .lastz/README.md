# Last Z survivor shooter Alliance Duel VS

# Monday - Mod Vehicle Boost

On Monday, in the VS Duel, points are awarded using wrenches, blueprints, and radar missions, open "Exterior Module" chests.

## Standard TIPS

### Resource gathering

All the wrenches and blueprints you collect during the week from:
* treasure chests;
* special weekly events;
* paid packs;
*DO NOT* use them immediately; *hold back*. Use all of them on Monday; this will give you a lot of VS points that will allow you to open the highest-level Duel treasure chest. Chests level 4 to 9 depending if you researce the Prime and Super Rewards technology of Alliance Recognition.

### Gather VS points

Organize youself to use them when they coincide with the same "Full Preparedness" event theme. So every task you complete counts toward both events, meaning double the points and double the loot!

### Complete Radar events

Radar events are updated every X hours, depending on the radar's skill level. This level increases as events are consumed and it is *not* equal to the radar building level. When you reach maximum storage capacity, any new events generated will be lost and the radar level will not increase. Generally, always make sure to complete all events.
Every Sunday, starting at 12:00 PM APO time, complete all radar events (gathering food, wood, diamonds, and Z coins), killing Boomers, etc., etc. *but* don't claim them immediately. When you complete an event, a pop-up with a treasure icon will appear. Don't touch/click them. The radar can store at least 30 events (depending on your HQ level).
Claim them rewards on Monday as soon as you wake up; this will earn you a lot of VS points for events completed the day before, exactly when they matter most! It's important, do it; you'll get more events in X hours.

## Advanced TIPS

### Gather VS points

Each day of Alliance Duel, there are always two "Full Preparedness"-themed events that allow you to open double chests. This usually occurs during the third and final themed event. In the first, you can earn enough points to open all "Full Preparedness" chests by attacking Boomers. In the final themed event, you can consume all your collected items and all your remaining fuel by attacking Boomers.
Why? For the following reasons:
* "Full Preparedness" is often a tough competition; surprise your opponent with a final rush so they don't have time to prepare an alternative plan to close the points gap.
* The "Lurking Danger" daily event is a mix of 5 Rallys and 30 Rallys, so depending on how many Rallys remain until you get the final reward (you have to!), you know how much fuel you'll still have to spend. In any case, you spend all your fuel this day. To get more fuel you can buy it or upgrade the hero Katrina, her third skill gives 100 additional fuel when she reaches 1 star, and 10% when she fills up when you reach four stars (hard to get at the beginning)

### Complete Radar events

To get more events per day, you can get the hero Laura, which is available without spending money. Her third ability, when you reach a star, allows you to get 5 extra radar events, and another 10 if you reach 10 stars (but again, it's difficult to get at first).
