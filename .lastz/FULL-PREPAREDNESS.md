# Full Preparedness Event

This event takes place every day and provides essential rewards for growing your account.
The goal is to open the three chests at the top every day, earning 18 Preparedness medals.
There are five types of event themes to earn medals:
1. Hero Progression
2. Army Expansion
3. Mod Vehicle Boost
4. Shelder Upgrade
5. Age of Science

Each day consists of six event themes, each lasting four hours. One of the above will occur twice in the same day. To collect 18 Preparedness medals, you must complete three event themes. If you miss an event theme because it falls at night in your time zone, you can make up for it.
During the VS days, the event theme that awards points for the VS will always take place twice, and will always be the third and final one of the day.

Let's see how to complete these event themes and earn all the Preparedness medals by optimizing the use of resources that will be used only on the day of the VS. The events are listed based on the amount of points they earn:
### Hero Progression
In this event theme, you must only use hero experience collected from building construction, exploration, and radar events. Only consume the experience needed to complete the event theme and earn 6 Preparedness medals. For level 100 heroes, you only need to level up one more time to complete it.

### Army Expansion
To complete this event theme, simply claim soldiers from the training camps. For HQs above level 25, simply claim them to complete this event theme. For HQs below level 24, you will need a handful of points to open the last chest; you can obtain them by training 20 more soldiers of your choice. Speed ​​up your training now so you don't forget to claim them later. Put other soldiers into training right away, otherwise you'll be forced to waste important boosts that should only be used on Friday and during the SvS event. If you forget to put them into training, do it at the beginning of the event theme, set a timer (important), and only boost with 15/20 minutes left. You'll have saved 3 * 3.5 hours of training boosts. You MUST ALWAYS have soldiers in training.
### Mod Vehicle Boost
This event theme must be completed only by killing boomers. Depending on your HQ level, you'll need to complete 10 (160 fuel) to 15 (240 fuel) rallies. The 3-star hero Kate allows you to earn 300 fuel throughout the day. Do not use wrences or blueprints under any circumstances; you must accumulate them and use them on Monday in the last event theme of the VS.

### Shelder Upgrade and Age of Science
Ignore them; earning points in this event theme except on Tuesdays/Wednesdays and Fridays wastes speedup.

## TIPS:

### Hero Progression
You absolutely don't need to use up all the experience you have at that moment; this is the event theme that will earn you the most points in Full Preparedness. In a week, you can accumulate up to 1G per week. If used at the right time, it's the key to winning the day. First, spend hero experience, look at your daily rank and figure out how many points separate you from the top spot. Do a simple calculation: for example, today the top spot is 100,000 points ahead of me, multiply the difference by 2,000. To get to first place, I'd have to spend 200,000 hero experience (going up about 2 levels). If I've accumulated enough experience in the past few days and this event theme isn't repeated on the same day, I'll consume 200M with 1 minute left until the end of the event. This means you won't give your opponent time to realize you've outmatched them and they won't be able to take countermeasures. There are people who monitor the progress of the VS, and every time you score points, they'll always score a little more than you, just enough to win.

### Army expansion
For HQs of 25 or higher, simply claim soldiers from the barracks. If you're close to the top players in the Full Preparedness rankings for the day, claim them 10-15 minutes before the end of the event theme to surprise your opponent. At high HQ levels above 26, you'll earn more than 500,000 points.

This appears twice on Monday. You complete one of these as described above with just the rallies, while in the other (you choose whether it's during the 3rd or 6th event theme) you use keys and blueprints.
